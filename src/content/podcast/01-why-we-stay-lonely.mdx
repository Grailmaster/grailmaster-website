## In this episode

We treat loneliness as a feeling. But structurally, it's a measurable property of
your personal network — how many people you're connected to, how those people are
connected to each other, and how much *signal* (support, novelty, meaning) actually
flows through those ties.

This episode introduces the **Social Connectivity Value (SCV)** and why isolation
behaves less like sadness and more like a circulatory problem.

### What we cover

- Why "just meet more people" is the wrong prescription
- The longevity data linking connection to lifespan
- The three levers of SCV: **reach, redundancy, and richness**
- A 20-minute exercise to map your own network this week
