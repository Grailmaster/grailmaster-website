## In this episode

Most dating advice optimizes the wrong variables. Using research from evolutionary
psychology and network science, we look at what genuinely predicts a second date —
and why *responsiveness* beats almost everything else.

### What we cover

- The difference between attraction and connection
- Why self-disclosure is a protocol, not a personality trait
- A debrief template to turn every date into data
